## 🔬 Research & Publications

### CHAIR — Persian Information Retrieval

My Master's research focused on improving information retrieval for **Persian question answering systems**.
The framework **CHAIR** (Contextual Hybrid Artificial Intelligence Retrieval) combines:

- BM25 (lexical)
- ParsBERT (semantic)
- Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) & Weighted Fusion
- Persian text preprocessing & vector representations

Evaluated on the **PQuAD dataset** with metrics: Precision, Recall, MRR, MAP, NDCG, F1.

### Current Research: Intelligent Business Agents

I'm exploring **LLM‑based agents** that support organizations through:

- Organizational Analysis
- Business Intelligence
- Intelligent Decision Support
- AI‑assisted Business Processes

Goal: move beyond chat interfaces into **useful, integrated decision‑support components**.

| # | Law | Rationale |
|---|-----|-----------|
| **1** | **One Main Path** – `Software Engineering + Product Development + AI/NLP` | Depth over breadth |
